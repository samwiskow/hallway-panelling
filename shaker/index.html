Linear MDF and mould blanks allow 30 mm extra per piece and 10 mm stock-end trimming. The 2400 mm cap schedule allows 10 mm extra per piece and only 5 mm stock-end trimming. Check stock lengths first and use the spare if needed. Corner projections and actual wall angles set the final end cuts.

Fit it to the room

  1. No backing sheet is needed. The existing wall forms the centre of each panel.
  2. Fit the 70 mm top rail from 918 to 988 mm above the floor. Fit the 12 mm cap above it. The 60 mm bottom rail starts at 95 mm.
  3. The radiator-wall bottom rails are full-length material allowances. Split or omit them locally around pipes. Check the upright positions against valves as well as radiator bodies.
  4. Use square butt joints for the grid. Check actual corner angles before cutting the cap ends.

Still to check on site

Measure both radiator floor gaps, the full valve and pipe positions, and the depth at the top of the skirting. Pipes run above the skirting: no skirting notches are included. The survey retains a small 20 × 10 mm closure difference, so check corner angles and final wall lengths.
