Each sheet supplies two rows. Hallway-only rows are 840 mm rough; rows with an entrance panel are 880 mm rough. Finish each labelled panel to its own 835 or 875 mm height. Width blanks allow 5 mm extra, plus saw loss and 5 mm stock-edge allowance. The PDF shows all seven sheet layouts.

Linear MDF and mould blanks allow 30 mm extra per piece and 10 mm stock-end trimming. The 2400 mm cap schedule allows 10 mm extra per piece and only 5 mm stock-end trimming. Check stock lengths first and use the spare if needed. Corner projections and actual wall angles set the final end cuts.

Fit it to the room

  1. Hallway panels run from 95 to 930 mm above floor. Entrance panels run from 80 to 955 mm. Standard caps add 55 mm plus a 15 mm lip.
  2. E1 uses one 70 mm-high, 9 mm-deep flat cap. Fit it directly to the wall above the panels; do not stack it over the 6 mm sheet.
  3. Order grooves parallel to the 2440 mm sheet direction. Do not rotate offcuts by 90 degrees. Restart the groove layout at door openings.
  4. Confirm usable sheet width. A 1200 mm panel cannot come from a 1200 mm sheet with trimming allowance.
  5. Template pipe, bracket and socket cutouts on site. Keep valve and wall-box access clear. Use suitable trade support for radiator or faceplate removal.
  6. Seven sheets fit the layout. No extra spare sheet is included. Allow extra material if groove alignment or a failed cutout requires it.

Still to check on site

Measure the hallway radiator floor gaps, all valve and pipe positions, the depth at the top of the skirting, and the barn-door stop position. Pipes run above the skirting: no skirting notches are included. The hallway survey retains a 20 × 10 mm closure difference. The entrance has a 5 mm width and depth difference. Check corner angles and final wall lengths.
