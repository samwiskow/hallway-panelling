Fit it to the room

  1. Use Wickes 121175 for the main dado and Wickes 121300 for all frames and the E1 dado. Fit both directly to the wall without a backing strip.
  2. Keep the dado top at 1000 mm in the hallway and 1025 mm in the entrance. The 29 mm main dado starts at 971 / 996 mm. The 28 mm E1 dado starts at 997 mm above the entrance floor; align the tops and fit the 1 mm bottom-edge change with a physical sample.
  3. Frames use the rounded marking dimensions: 700 mm high in the hallway, from 190 to 890 mm above the floor, and 800 mm in the entrance, from 110 to 910 mm. All widths use 10 mm steps and include the 28 mm moulding.
  4. E1 uses the 9 mm-deep 3 Rise moulding for both the frame and dado. This leaves 6 mm within the 15 mm door gap before adhesive and wall variation. The 15 mm cover moulding has no working clearance there. Dry-fit through the full door travel.
  5. The entrance lower frame member runs from 110 to 138 mm above the floor. Check the estimated 150 mm pipe entries, measured 160 mm bracket bottom and all valve clearances.
  6. Frame cuts are measured from outside long point to outside long point. Do not subtract the moulding width. Both ends are mitred.
  7. One full frame continues behind each radiator. Check pipes, valves and brackets, and leave local gaps where needed. Radiator removal may need trade support.
  8. Left edges are measured from the clockwise start of the whole numbered wall. Use the marking dimensions for frame positions and gaps; do not make unequal frames the same width.

Still to check on site

Measure the hallway radiator floor gaps, all valve and pipe positions, the depth at the top of the skirting, and the barn-door stop position. Pipes run above the skirting: no skirting notches are included. The hallway survey retains a 20 × 10 mm closure difference. The entrance has a 5 mm width and depth difference. Check corner angles and final wall lengths.
