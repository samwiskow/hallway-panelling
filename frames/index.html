Linear MDF and mould blanks allow 30 mm extra per piece and 10 mm stock-end trimming. The 2400 mm cap schedule allows 10 mm extra per piece and only 5 mm stock-end trimming. Check stock lengths first and use the spare if needed. Corner projections and actual wall angles set the final end cuts.

Fit it to the room

  1. Select a physical moulding sample before cutting. One shaped dado replaces the small profile layers shown in the render.
  2. Set the frame bottoms at 185 mm and their tops at 890 mm above the floor. The dado starts at 950 mm.
  3. Frame cuts are measured from outside long point to outside long point. Do not subtract the moulding width. Both ends are mitred.
  4. Keep the wall behind each radiator plain. Check nearby frame positions against the valves and pipes. Dry-fit one complete frame first.
  5. Left-edge positions in the table are measured from the clockwise start of the whole numbered wall. Decimal sizes come from the layout; set equal frame widths on site.

Still to check on site

Measure both radiator floor gaps, the full valve and pipe positions, and the depth at the top of the skirting. Pipes run above the skirting: no skirting notches are included. The survey retains a small 20 × 10 mm closure difference, so check corner angles and final wall lengths.
